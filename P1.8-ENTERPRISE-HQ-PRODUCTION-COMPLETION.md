# Anaira P1.8 — Enterprise HQ Production Completion

Built on the P1.4 production ZIP and existing Phase 7 enterprise schema. Existing business data is preserved.

## Delivered
- Multi-outlet HQ dashboard with period KPIs and outlet network.
- Outlet comparison from enterprise daily snapshots.
- Central menu catalog management.
- Outlet-specific central pricing with upsert API.
- Enterprise inventory transfer request → approval → receive workflow.
- Real outlet stock deduction/addition during transfer receive, with inventory movements and audit log.
- Enterprise approval center decision API.
- Staff movement request workflow with idempotency.
- Consolidated outlet/group sales and expense reports.
- Group accounting view over existing P0 accounting journal entries.
- Enterprise device/target/audit visibility.
- Enterprise HQ navigation in the dashboard sidebar.
- RLS for staff movement and existing enterprise membership isolation.
- Idempotency on inventory transfers and staff movement requests.

## Important safety behavior
- No business rows are deleted or reset.
- Enterprise actions are membership-checked server-side and in SECURITY DEFINER RPCs.
- Inventory transfer cannot receive without a valid source inventory item and sufficient stock.
- A transfer cannot be duplicated with the same enterprise/idempotency key.
- Existing Restaurant Suite and payment systems remain separate.
