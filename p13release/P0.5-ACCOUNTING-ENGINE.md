# P0.5 Accounting Engine

Built on P0.4. Adds a double-entry accounting journal and controlled synchronization from existing POS financial sources.

## Sources
- Orders and paid order payments
- Refunds
- Expenses
- Purchase orders
- Supplier payments

## Accounts
- 1000 Cash
- 1010 Bank / Digital Payments
- 1090 Other Receivables
- 1200 Inventory / Purchases
- 2000 Supplier Payable
- 2100 Tax Payable
- 4000 Food & Beverage Sales
- 4010 Service Charges
- 4090 Sales Returns
- 5000 Operating Expenses

## Controls
- Restaurant-scoped RPC access
- Existing data is read and journaled; source business rows are not deleted
- Unique source/account/amount guard prevents duplicate postings
- Journal report checks debit vs credit
- Date-range sync is repeatable

## Important
This phase creates an accounting layer and export-ready ledger foundation. It does not silently alter historical POS transactions or claim direct Tally/Zoho/QuickBooks API connectivity without provider credentials/configuration.
